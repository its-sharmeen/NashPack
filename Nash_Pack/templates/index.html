AUTOMATED LABEL VERIFICATION

Every packet makes seven promises. This checks that it kept them.

Under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, every pre-packed item sold in India must declare who made it, how much is inside, what it costs, when it was packed, and who to call if something's wrong. Manually checking that across a market's worth of packets is what this tool exists to speed up: scan a label photo and get a declaration-by-declaration finding in seconds.
